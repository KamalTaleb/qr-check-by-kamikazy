# QR Check by Kamikazy: privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

QR Check does not collect, store, send or sell any personal data.

- The link you type and the QR code made from it stay on your computer. The extension makes no network requests.
- The "This page" button reads the address of the tab you are on, only when you click it, and only to put it in the link box. This uses Chrome's `activeTab` permission.
- Your style choices (colours, error correction, PNG size) are saved in your browser's local storage so they are there next time. They never leave your computer.
- No analytics, no trackers, no accounts.

Questions: https://github.com/KamalTaleb/qr-check-by-kamikazy/issues
